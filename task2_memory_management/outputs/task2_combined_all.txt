=== DEMO 3: LRU page replacement ===
Requirement 2.1(2), second algorithm

Policy: evict the page UNUSED for the longest time, because recent
        use is the best simple predictor of imminent use.

    1 frame(s): 12 faults
    2 frame(s): 12 faults
    3 frame(s): 10 faults
    4 frame(s):  8 faults
    5 frame(s):  5 faults
  No anomaly, as theory predicts: LRU is a stack algorithm, so the

  Conclusion: which policy wins is a property of the ACCESS PATTERN,
  not of the algorithm alone. LRU pays off where a hot working set is
  re-read while other pages stream past; where there is no locality the
  policies converge and FIFO's much lower cost decides it. Only FIFO
  can suffer Belady's anomaly. CLOCK tracks LRU at nearly FIFO's cost,
  which is why it is what production kernels actually implement.
