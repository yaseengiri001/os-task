Summary
  Passwords are never written anywhere. Per-user salts make
  precomputed rainbow tables useless and hide shared passwords.
  200000 PBKDF2 rounds impose the same cost on every attacker guess.
  Comparison is constant-time, failures are indistinguishable, and
  repeated guessing locks the account.
